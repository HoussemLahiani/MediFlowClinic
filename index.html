MediFlow: Clinic Manager

MediFlow: Clinic Manager is a modern clinic management application designed for physiotherapists and rehabilitation professionals. It helps healthcare providers organize their daily workflow, manage patients, schedule appointments, monitor treatments, and generate professional reports from a single secure application.

Our goal is to provide a fast, secure, and intuitive solution that allows healthcare professionals to focus on patient care instead of administrative tasks.

  • 👨‍⚕️ Complete patient management
  • 📅 Appointment scheduling
  • 📝 Treatment session tracking
  • 💰 Income and payment management

  • 📊 Advanced business analytics (Premium)

Privacy First

Your patient data belongs to you. MediFlow stores your information locally on your device. If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, your encrypted backup files are stored only in your own Google Drive account. MediFlow does not store your medical data on its own servers.

Google Account Integration

When enabled, MediFlow uses your Google account only to:

  • Sign you in securely
  • Access your private Google Drive application folder
  • Upload and restore your backup files

MediFlow never accesses your personal Google Drive files outside its dedicated application folder.

Medical Disclaimer

The application is intended exclusively for professional clinic management and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
