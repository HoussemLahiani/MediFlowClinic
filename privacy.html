Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

MediFlow: Clinic Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and protected when you use our application.

1. Data Ownership and Local Storage

Your patient data belongs entirely to you. MediFlow stores all your clinical records, patient profiles, appointment details, treatment sessions, and financial records locally on your device. We do not store, process, or transmit your medical data to external servers owned by us.

2. Google Drive Integration & Scopes

MediFlow offers an optional cloud backup feature using Google Drive. When you enable this feature, the application asks for permission to access your Google Account.

MediFlow uses restricted Google OAuth scopes exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity securely via Google Sign-In.
  • Access a dedicated, private application folder within your personal Google Drive space (`appDataFolder`).
  • Upload, update, and restore your encrypted application database backups.

Important: MediFlow never accesses, views, alters, or deletes your personal Google Drive files outside of the application's dedicated folder.

3. Medical Disclaimer

MediFlow is an administrative tool designed strictly for clinic management, scheduling, and billing tracking. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical advice, or treatment recommendations.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal or clinical data to third parties. All data generated within the app remains strictly on your device or in your personal Google Drive account.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support option available within the application.
